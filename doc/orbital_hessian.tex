\section*{Conventions}

One-particle and two-particle reduced density matrices:
\begin{align}
D_{ab} &= \langle 0 | \ccr{a}\ann{b} | 0\rangle, \\
\Gamma_{pqrs} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\langle 0 | \ccr{p}\ccr{q}\ann{s}\ann{r} | 0\rangle .
\end{align}

Two-electron integrals in physicist's notation, $\langle ik|jl\rangle$.

Hamiltonian:
\begin{equation}
\hat H = \hat H_1 + \hat H_2, \qquad
\hat H_1 = \sum_{pq} h_{pq}\,\ccr{p}\ann{q}, \qquad
\hat H_2 = \tfrac12 \sum_{uvwx} \langle uv|wx\rangle\, \ccr{u}\ccr{v}\ann{x}\ann{w}.
\end{equation}

Orbital rotation generator (anti-Hermitian): $\hat\kappa = \sum_{pq}\kappa_{pq}\,\ccr{p}\ann{q}$,
with $\kappa_{qp} = -\kappa_{pq}^{*}$.

\section*{Generalized Fock matrix}

\begin{equation}
F_{qp} \;=\; \sum_{r} D_{rq}\,h_{rp} \;+\; 2\sum_{rst} \Gamma_{srtq}\,\langle sr|tp\rangle .
\end{equation}

\section*{Gradient}

\begin{equation}
g_{pq} \;=\; F_{qp} \;-\; F_{pq}^{*}.
\end{equation}

\section*{Hessian}

Energy expansion:
\begin{equation}
E(\boldsymbol\kappa) \approx E(\mathbf 0) + \sum_{pq}\kappa_{pq}\,g_{pq}
+ \tfrac12 \sum_{pq,rs}\kappa_{pq}\,\kappa_{rs}\,G_{pq,rs} + \cdots,
\qquad
G_{pq,rs} = \langle 0|\,[[\hat H,\ccr{p}\ann{q}],\ccr{r}\ann{s}]\,|0\rangle .
\end{equation}

\subsection*{One-electron contribution}

\begin{equation}
G^{(1)}_{pq,rs} \;=\; \delta_{qr}\sum_{u} h_{up}\,D_{us}
\;-\; h_{sp}\,D_{rq}
\;-\; h_{qr}\,D_{ps}
\;+\; \delta_{sp}\sum_{v} h_{qv}\,D_{rv}.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Two-electron contribution}

\begin{align}
G^{(2)}_{pq,rs} \;=\;
&+\,2\,\delta_{rq}\sum_{uvt} \langle uv|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvst}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uv} \langle uv|pr\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvsq} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{ut} \langle us|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt}
\;-\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle su|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{ut} \langle uq|rt\rangle\,\Gamma_{pust}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uw} \langle uq|wr\rangle\,\Gamma_{pusw} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{wt} \langle sq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{rpwt}
\;-\; 2\,\delta_{sp}\sum_{uwt} \langle uq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruwt}.
\end{align}

\subsection*{Full Hessian, Fock-matrix form}

The $\delta_{qr}$ and $\delta_{sp}$ terms of $G^{(1)}$ and $G^{(2)}$ combine exactly into
$F_{sp}$ and $F_{rq}^{*}$, leaving six two-electron ``exchange-type'' terms that do not
reduce to $F$:

\begin{align}
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
G_{pq,rs} \;=\;\;
& \delta_{qr}\,F_{sp} \;+\; \delta_{sp}\,F_{rq}^{*}
\;-\; h_{sp}\,D_{rq} \;-\; h_{qr}\,D_{ps} \\[4pt]
& -\,2\sum_{uv} \langle uv|pr\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvsq}
\;+\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle us|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt}
\;-\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle su|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt} \\[4pt]
& +\,2\sum_{ut} \langle uq|rt\rangle\,\Gamma_{pust}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uw} \langle uq|wr\rangle\,\Gamma_{pusw}
\;+\; 2\sum_{wt} \langle sq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{rpwt}
\end{aligned}}
\label{eq:g-boxed}
\end{align}

\section*{Real/imaginary directional blocks}

For a complex (relativistic, spin-orbit-coupled) spinor basis, $\kappa_{pq}$
for an independent pair $p>q$ is a free complex number, $\kappa_{pq}=t_{pq}+iy_{pq}$
($\kappa_{qp}=-t_{pq}+iy_{pq}$, enforcing $\kappa_{qp}=-\kappa_{pq}^{*}$). The full
real Hessian over these $2\times(\text{number of pairs})$ real parameters therefore
has three independent blocks between any two distinct pairs $(p,q)$ and $(r,s)$, all
obtainable from the SAME four raw terms $G_{pq,rs}$, $G_{pq,sr}$, $G_{qp,rs}$,
$G_{qp,sr}$ by substituting $\kappa_{pq}=t+iy$, $\kappa_{qp}=-t+iy$,
$\kappa_{rs}=t'+iy'$, $\kappa_{sr}=-t'+iy'$ into
$\tfrac12\sum\kappa_{pq}\kappa_{rs}G_{pq,rs}$ and reading off the coefficient of
each real product:

\begin{align}
\text{Hess}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial t_{pq}\,\partial t_{rs}}
= G_{pq,rs} - G_{pq,sr} - G_{qp,rs} + G_{qp,sr}, \label{eq:hess-real} \\[4pt]
\text{Hess}^{yy}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial y_{pq}\,\partial y_{rs}}
= -\big(G_{pq,rs} + G_{pq,sr} + G_{qp,rs} + G_{qp,sr}\big), \\[4pt]
\text{Hess}^{ty}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial t_{pq}\,\partial y_{rs}}
= i\big(G_{rs,pq} + G_{rs,qp} - G_{sr,pq} - G_{sr,qp}\big).
\end{align}

The first two are the $(+,-,-,+)$ and $(-,-,-,-)$ sign patterns over the four raw
terms with $(p,q)$ in the flipping (first) slot; the third is the same idea but
with the \emph{other} pair, $(r,s)$, in the flipping slot -- not $(p,q)$, which is
easy to get backwards by analogy with the first two (an earlier draft of this
derivation did exactly that, and was only caught by the finite-difference check
below). The fourth possible direction, $\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}=\partial^2E/\partial
y_{pq}\partial t_{rs}$, is not a new formula: by Schwarz's theorem,
$\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}=\text{Hess}^{ty}_{rs,pq}$.

All three are real for physical (Hermitian $h$, $\langle\cdot|\cdot\rangle$, $D$,
$\Gamma$) input by the same argument: $K_{pq}=\ccr{p}\ann{q}-\ccr{q}\ann{p}$ and
$K'_{rs}=i(\ccr{r}\ann{s}+\ccr{s}\ann{r})$ are both anti-Hermitian, so
$[[\hat H,K_{pq}],K'_{rs}]$ is Hermitian and its expectation value is real,
regardless of which of the three sign patterns above it corresponds to.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Status note (updated).} $G^{(1)}_{pq,rs}$, the gradient $g_{pq}$,
$\text{Hess}_{pq,rs}$, and $\text{Hess}^{yy}_{pq,rs}$ have all been independently
verified: by more than one derivation route, by an explicit small-Fock-space
numerical check against a general (non-idempotent, non-Wick-derivable) random
1-/2-RDM, and by finite-difference checks against converged NON\_REL/C4\_DHF SCF
data. $\text{Hess}^{ty}_{pq,rs}$ (and hence $\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}$ via Schwarz)
has since also been derived and confirmed the same way -- a genuine
mixed-direction (real step on one pair, imaginary step on the other) 2D finite
difference against water/STO-3G's converged AND a deliberately non-converged
(1 SCF iteration) C4\_DHF density, matching to $O(\text{step}^2)$ in both cases
(implementation: \texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeHessian.h}'s
\texttt{hartreeExchangeHessianElementMixed}, the Hartree/exchange-ansatz "cheap"
path only -- the fully general (\texttt{GeneralizedHessian.h}) counterpart has not
been added). The only remaining unimplemented piece for a full $2N(N-1)$-real-parameter
orbital-rotation Hessian is the general (non-Hartree/exchange-ansatz) 2-RDM version
of the mixed block, and assembling all three blocks into one joint matrix for an
actual Newton-Raphson/NEO step (currently only $\text{Hess}_{pq,rs}$ alone is ever
assembled into a diagonalizable matrix, for the minimum/saddle-point diagnostic in
\texttt{main.cpp}'s \texttt{HESSIAN\_4C}).

\section{Cheap ansatz for PNOF functionals: $G_{pq,rs}$ in terms of
two\_rdm\_H, two\_rdm\_X, and the pair terms L1/L2}
\label{sec:pnof-hessian}

\subsection*{The ansatz}

Every PNOF functional this project implements (\texttt{Hessian\_opt/PnofFock.h})
is handled by the SAME cheap diagonal-$D$ 2-RDM ansatz already used for
the ordinary Hartree/exchange gradient (\texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeGradient.h}),
extended with two further ``pair'' terms:
\begin{equation}
\Gamma_{pqrs} \;=\;
\tfrac12\Big[\mathrm{H}_{pq}\,\delta_{pr}\delta_{qs} - \mathrm{X}_{pq}\,\delta_{ps}\delta_{qr}\Big]
\;+\; \delta_{q,\bar p}\,\delta_{s,\bar r}\Big[L^{1}_{pr} + L^{2}_{p\bar r}\Big],
\label{eq:pnof-ansatz}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{H}_{pq}\equiv\texttt{two\_rdm\_h}(p,q)$, $\mathrm{X}_{pq}\equiv
\texttt{two\_rdm\_x}(p,q)$, $L^{1}_{pq}\equiv\texttt{two\_rdm\_l1}(p,q)$,
$L^{2}_{pq}\equiv\texttt{two\_rdm\_l2}(p,q)$ (all real, independent of the
orbital basis being real or complex), and $\bar p\equiv\texttt{pair\_of}(p)$
is $p$'s Kramers/spin partner. Equation~\eqref{eq:pnof-ansatz} says: the
plain Hartree/exchange part fires at $(p,q,r,s)=(p,q,p,q)$ and $(p,q,q,p)$
as usual; the pair part fires ONLY when $q=\bar p$ AND $s=\bar r$ (i.e.\ the
first PAIR $(p,q)$ and the third/fourth PAIR $(r,s)$ are each a Kramers/spin
pair themselves), where it evaluates to $L^{1}_{pr}+L^{2}_{p\bar r}$ --
BOTH $L^1$ and $L^2$ contribute at that SAME tensor entry, evaluated at
different index pairs ($L^1$ at $(p,r)$, $L^2$ at $(p,\bar r)$), exactly
as\\ \texttt{HartreeExchangeGradient.cpp}'s own $F_{pq}$ derivation already
uses them. For a PNOF functional, $\mathrm{H}_{pq}=\mathrm{X}_{pq}$
(uniform across every Kramers/spin bar-combination of a geminal pair,
doc/rel\_pnofs.tex's own \texttt{eq:coulomb-elems}) and $L^{1}_{pq}=
-L^{2}_{pq}=\pm\Pi_{\text{rep}(p),\text{rep}(q)}/4$ (sign $+$ at matching
bar-parity, $-$ at mismatched). The coefficient is $\Pi/4$, not $\Pi/2$,
because $L^1$ and $L^2$ BOTH contribute at the same tensor entry
(Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-ansatz}): $\Gamma_{p\bar p r\bar r}=L^1_{pr}+L^2_{p\bar r}=
\pm\Pi/2$, the value of doc/rel\_pnofs.tex's Eqs.~98c--f. (An earlier
version stored $\pm\Pi/2$ in each, i.e.\ $\Gamma=\pm\Pi$, which doubled the
pairing energy: it disagreed with \texttt{Occ\_opt/PNOFs.h}'s
\texttt{pnofElectronicEnergy}, itself equal to the exact two-electron
pair-CI energy, by $3.9\times10^{-2}$~Hartree for H$_2$/cc-pVTZ PNOF5.
Corrected 2026-09-20; energy, generalized Fock matrix and Hessian, all
linear in $L^{1,2}$, are fixed together.) See \texttt{PnofFock.h}'s own
header comment for the derivation from Eqs.~98c--f.

\subsection*{Hartree/exchange contribution to $G_{pq,rs}$}

Substituting only the $\mathrm{H}/\mathrm{X}$ part of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-ansatz} into the boxed $G_{pq,rs}$ of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:g-boxed} (already implemented,
\texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeHessian.h}'s \texttt{hartreeExchangeHessianElement},
before this section's own L1/L2 extension) collapses every double sum
there to a single one, exactly as\\ \texttt{hartreeExchangeFockMatrix}'s own
gradient derivation already does for $F_{pq}$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
G^{\mathrm{HX}}_{pq,rs} \;=\;\;
& \delta_{qr}\,F_{sp} \;+\; \delta_{sp}\,F^{*}_{rq}
\;-\; \delta_{qr}\,h_{sp}\,D_{rq} \;-\; \delta_{sp}\,h_{qr}\,D_{ps} \\[4pt]
& +\,\delta_{qr}\sum_{u}\mathrm{H}_{ru}\big[\langle us|pu\rangle-\langle su|pu\rangle\big]
\;+\;\delta_{sp}\sum_{u}\mathrm{H}_{pu}\big[\langle uq|ru\rangle-\langle uq|ur\rangle\big] \\[4pt]
& +\,\langle sq|pr\rangle\Big[\mathrm{X}_{rq}-\mathrm{H}_{sq}+\mathrm{X}_{ps}-\mathrm{X}_{rp}\Big]
\;+\,\langle sq|rp\rangle\Big[\mathrm{H}_{rp}+\mathrm{X}_{qs}-\mathrm{X}_{ps}-\mathrm{X}_{rq}\Big].
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pnof-hx-term}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Pair (L1/L2) contribution to $G_{pq,rs}$}

Substituting only the pair part of Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-ansatz} into the
SAME six two-electron sums of the boxed $G_{pq,rs}$ and collapsing each
double sum via its own $\delta_{q,\bar p}\delta_{s,\bar r}$-type
constraint (four of the six collapse completely to a SINGLE eri times a
SINGLE $L^1+L^2$ coefficient; the other two collapse only conditionally,
leaving one remaining sum, exactly when the OUTER indices already satisfy
the pair condition) gives a genuinely new contribution, not obtainable
from Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-hx-term} alone:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
G^{\mathrm{L}}_{pq,rs} \;=\;\;
& -\,2\,[q=\bar s]\sum_{u}\langle u\bar u|pr\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{us}+L^{2}_{uq}\Big] \\[4pt]
& +\,2\langle \bar r s|p\bar q\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{rq}+L^{2}_{r\bar q}\Big]
\;-\,2\langle s\bar r|p\bar q\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{rq}+L^{2}_{r\bar q}\Big] \\[4pt]
& +\,2\langle \bar p q|r\bar s\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{ps}+L^{2}_{p\bar s}\Big]
\;-\,2\langle \bar p q|\bar s r\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{ps}+L^{2}_{p\bar s}\Big] \\[4pt]
& +\,2\,[p=\bar r]\sum_{w}\langle sq|w\bar w\rangle\Big[L^{1}_{rw}+L^{2}_{r\bar w}\Big],
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:pnof-l1l2-term}
\end{equation}
where $[\,\cdot\,]$ is the Iverson bracket (1 if the condition holds, 0
otherwise) -- the first and last lines vanish identically unless the
OUTER pair $(q,s)$ or $(p,r)$ already happens to satisfy the SAME
Kramers/spin-pair relation the ansatz itself requires, in which case a
single sum over the remaining free index survives; the middle two lines
(four raw terms, pairwise sharing the SAME $L^1+L^2$ coefficient) are
UNCONDITIONAL, contributing to every $(p,q,r,s)$.

\subsection*{Combined result}

\begin{equation}
G_{pq,rs} \;=\; G^{\mathrm{HX}}_{pq,rs} \;+\; G^{\mathrm{L}}_{pq,rs},
\label{eq:pnof-full-g}
\end{equation}
and the real-step Hessian follows exactly as in Eq.~\eqref{eq:hess-real}
(this file's own $(+,-,-,+)$ antisymmetrization over the two pairs, applied
to the COMBINED $G_{pq,rs}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-full-g}, not to
$G^{\mathrm{HX}}$ and $G^{\mathrm{L}}$ separately -- though since the
antisymmetrization is linear, applying it to each piece and adding is
equivalent, which is exactly how \texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeHessian.cpp}'s
own implementation is structured, two independent raw-term helpers summed
before/after antisymmetrizing).

\subsection*{Status}

Implemented in \texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeHessian.h}'s
\texttt{hartreeExchangeHessianElement} (optional \texttt{pair\_of}/
\texttt{two\_rdm\_l1}/\texttt{two\_rdm\_l2} arguments, default empty
reducing to Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-hx-term} alone) and wired into PNOF via
\texttt{Hessian\_opt/PnofHessian.h}. Only the REAL-step block
$\text{Hess}_{pq,rs}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:hess-real}) has this L1/L2
extension so far -- $\text{Hess}^{yy}_{pq,rs}$ and
$\text{Hess}^{ty}_{pq,rs}$ do not yet. Validated two ways: (i) against
\texttt{GeneralizedHessian.h}'s fully general \texttt{generalizedOrbitalHessianElement},
fed an explicit dense $\Gamma$ built from the SAME Eq.~\eqref{eq:pnof-ansatz}
ansatz, on random data ($T=$ real and complex, $\mathrm{H}$/$\mathrm{X}$/
$L^1$/$L^2$ deliberately unequal and unrelated to any occupation-number
formula) -- machine precision across every tested $(p,q,r,s)$ quadruple;
(ii) against a genuine e$^{\boldsymbol\kappa}$ finite-difference probe
(\texttt{Hessian\_opt/OrbitalRotationFiniteDifference.h}) on real,
converged LiH/6-31G SCF data for BOTH the X2C and 4-component (C4\_DHF)
paths -- machine precision ($\sim 10^{-14}$) there too, confirmed
robust across multiple $(p,q,r,s)$ quadruple choices including ones
where $(r,s)$ are $(p,q)$'s own Kramers-bar-partners.

Before this L1/L2 extension existed, PNOF's own \texttt{PnofFock.h}
instead folded $\Pi$ (the geminal pairing coefficient) directly into
$\mathrm{X}_{pq}$ and used no L1/L2 terms at all. That simplification is
EXACTLY energy- and Fock-equivalent for a Kramers-symmetric spinor basis
(an exact integral identity, $\langle p\bar p|q\bar q\rangle=\langle
pq|qp\rangle$ and $\langle p\bar p|\bar q q\rangle=-\langle\bar p q|q\bar
p\rangle$, confirmed numerically to machine precision) but is NOT
Hessian-equivalent: $G_{pq,rs}$ fixes all four indices explicitly, so it
sees the difference between weight sitting at the $\mathrm{X}_{pq}$
tensor position versus the $L^{1}_{pq}$/$L^{2}_{pq}$ one, even though
$F_{pq}$ and $E$ do not. This is why the L1/L2 extension above is
necessary, not merely convenient, for a correct PNOF Hessian.
