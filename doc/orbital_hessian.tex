\section*{Conventions}

One-particle and two-particle reduced density matrices:
\begin{align}
D_{ab} &= \langle 0 | \ccr{a}\ann{b} | 0\rangle, \\
\Gamma_{pqrs} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\langle 0 | \ccr{p}\ccr{q}\ann{s}\ann{r} | 0\rangle .
\end{align}

Two-electron integrals in physicist's notation, $\langle ik|jl\rangle$.

Hamiltonian:
\begin{equation}
\hat H = \hat H_1 + \hat H_2, \qquad
\hat H_1 = \sum_{pq} h_{pq}\,\ccr{p}\ann{q}, \qquad
\hat H_2 = \tfrac12 \sum_{uvwx} \langle uv|wx\rangle\, \ccr{u}\ccr{v}\ann{x}\ann{w}.
\end{equation}

Orbital rotation generator (anti-Hermitian): $\hat\kappa = \sum_{pq}\kappa_{pq}\,\ccr{p}\ann{q}$,
with $\kappa_{qp} = -\kappa_{pq}^{*}$.

\section*{Generalized Fock matrix}

\begin{equation}
F_{qp} \;=\; \sum_{r} D_{rq}\,h_{rp} \;+\; 2\sum_{rst} \Gamma_{srtq}\,\langle sr|tp\rangle .
\end{equation}

\section*{Gradient}

\begin{equation}
g_{pq} \;=\; F_{qp} \;-\; F_{pq}^{*}.
\end{equation}

\section*{Hessian}

Energy expansion:
\begin{equation}
E(\boldsymbol\kappa) \approx E(\mathbf 0) + \sum_{pq}\kappa_{pq}\,g_{pq}
+ \tfrac12 \sum_{pq,rs}\kappa_{pq}\,\kappa_{rs}\,G_{pq,rs} + \cdots,
\qquad
G_{pq,rs} = \langle 0|\,[[\hat H,\ccr{p}\ann{q}],\ccr{r}\ann{s}]\,|0\rangle .
\end{equation}

\subsection*{One-electron contribution}

\begin{equation}
G^{(1)}_{pq,rs} \;=\; \delta_{qr}\sum_{u} h_{up}\,D_{us}
\;-\; h_{sp}\,D_{rq}
\;-\; h_{qr}\,D_{ps}
\;+\; \delta_{sp}\sum_{v} h_{qv}\,D_{rv}.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Two-electron contribution}

\begin{align}
G^{(2)}_{pq,rs} \;=\;
&+\,2\,\delta_{rq}\sum_{uvt} \langle uv|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvst}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uv} \langle uv|pr\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvsq} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{ut} \langle us|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt}
\;-\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle su|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{ut} \langle uq|rt\rangle\,\Gamma_{pust}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uw} \langle uq|wr\rangle\,\Gamma_{pusw} \notag\\
&+\,2\sum_{wt} \langle sq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{rpwt}
\;-\; 2\,\delta_{sp}\sum_{uwt} \langle uq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruwt}.
\end{align}

\subsection*{Full Hessian, Fock-matrix form}

The $\delta_{qr}$ and $\delta_{sp}$ terms of $G^{(1)}$ and $G^{(2)}$ combine exactly into
$F_{sp}$ and $F_{rq}^{*}$, leaving six two-electron ``exchange-type'' terms that do not
reduce to $F$:

\begin{align}
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
G_{pq,rs} \;=\;\;
& \delta_{qr}\,F_{sp} \;+\; \delta_{sp}\,F_{rq}^{*}
\;-\; h_{sp}\,D_{rq} \;-\; h_{qr}\,D_{ps} \\[4pt]
& -\,2\sum_{uv} \langle uv|pr\rangle\,\Gamma_{uvsq}
\;+\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle us|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt}
\;-\; 2\sum_{ut} \langle su|pt\rangle\,\Gamma_{ruqt} \\[4pt]
& +\,2\sum_{ut} \langle uq|rt\rangle\,\Gamma_{pust}
\;-\; 2\sum_{uw} \langle uq|wr\rangle\,\Gamma_{pusw}
\;+\; 2\sum_{wt} \langle sq|wt\rangle\,\Gamma_{rpwt}
\end{aligned}}
\end{align}

\section*{Real/imaginary directional blocks}

For a complex (relativistic, spin-orbit-coupled) spinor basis, $\kappa_{pq}$
for an independent pair $p>q$ is a free complex number, $\kappa_{pq}=t_{pq}+iy_{pq}$
($\kappa_{qp}=-t_{pq}+iy_{pq}$, enforcing $\kappa_{qp}=-\kappa_{pq}^{*}$). The full
real Hessian over these $2\times(\text{number of pairs})$ real parameters therefore
has three independent blocks between any two distinct pairs $(p,q)$ and $(r,s)$, all
obtainable from the SAME four raw terms $G_{pq,rs}$, $G_{pq,sr}$, $G_{qp,rs}$,
$G_{qp,sr}$ by substituting $\kappa_{pq}=t+iy$, $\kappa_{qp}=-t+iy$,
$\kappa_{rs}=t'+iy'$, $\kappa_{sr}=-t'+iy'$ into
$\tfrac12\sum\kappa_{pq}\kappa_{rs}G_{pq,rs}$ and reading off the coefficient of
each real product:

\begin{align}
\text{Hess}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial t_{pq}\,\partial t_{rs}}
= G_{pq,rs} - G_{pq,sr} - G_{qp,rs} + G_{qp,sr}, \\[4pt]
\text{Hess}^{yy}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial y_{pq}\,\partial y_{rs}}
= -\big(G_{pq,rs} + G_{pq,sr} + G_{qp,rs} + G_{qp,sr}\big), \\[4pt]
\text{Hess}^{ty}_{pq,rs} &= \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial t_{pq}\,\partial y_{rs}}
= i\big(G_{rs,pq} + G_{rs,qp} - G_{sr,pq} - G_{sr,qp}\big).
\end{align}

The first two are the $(+,-,-,+)$ and $(-,-,-,-)$ sign patterns over the four raw
terms with $(p,q)$ in the flipping (first) slot; the third is the same idea but
with the \emph{other} pair, $(r,s)$, in the flipping slot -- not $(p,q)$, which is
easy to get backwards by analogy with the first two (an earlier draft of this
derivation did exactly that, and was only caught by the finite-difference check
below). The fourth possible direction, $\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}=\partial^2E/\partial
y_{pq}\partial t_{rs}$, is not a new formula: by Schwarz's theorem,
$\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}=\text{Hess}^{ty}_{rs,pq}$.

All three are real for physical (Hermitian $h$, $\langle\cdot|\cdot\rangle$, $D$,
$\Gamma$) input by the same argument: $K_{pq}=\ccr{p}\ann{q}-\ccr{q}\ann{p}$ and
$K'_{rs}=i(\ccr{r}\ann{s}+\ccr{s}\ann{r})$ are both anti-Hermitian, so
$[[\hat H,K_{pq}],K'_{rs}]$ is Hermitian and its expectation value is real,
regardless of which of the three sign patterns above it corresponds to.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Status note (updated).} $G^{(1)}_{pq,rs}$, the gradient $g_{pq}$,
$\text{Hess}_{pq,rs}$, and $\text{Hess}^{yy}_{pq,rs}$ have all been independently
verified: by more than one derivation route, by an explicit small-Fock-space
numerical check against a general (non-idempotent, non-Wick-derivable) random
1-/2-RDM, and by finite-difference checks against converged NON\_REL/C4\_DHF SCF
data. $\text{Hess}^{ty}_{pq,rs}$ (and hence $\text{Hess}^{yt}_{pq,rs}$ via Schwarz)
has since also been derived and confirmed the same way -- a genuine
mixed-direction (real step on one pair, imaginary step on the other) 2D finite
difference against water/STO-3G's converged AND a deliberately non-converged
(1 SCF iteration) C4\_DHF density, matching to $O(\text{step}^2)$ in both cases
(implementation: \texttt{Hessian\_opt/HartreeExchangeHessian.h}'s
\texttt{hartreeExchangeHessianElementMixed}, the Hartree/exchange-ansatz "cheap"
path only -- the fully general (\texttt{GeneralizedHessian.h}) counterpart has not
been added). The only remaining unimplemented piece for a full $2N(N-1)$-real-parameter
orbital-rotation Hessian is the general (non-Hartree/exchange-ansatz) 2-RDM version
of the mixed block, and assembling all three blocks into one joint matrix for an
actual Newton-Raphson/NEO step (currently only $\text{Hess}_{pq,rs}$ alone is ever
assembled into a diagonalizable matrix, for the minimum/saddle-point diagnostic in
\texttt{main.cpp}'s \texttt{HESSIAN\_4C}).
